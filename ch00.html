Start Here: How to Use This Notebook

How to study, the source policy, which chapters the teacher marked important, and which lecture covers which chapter.

1 · What this notebook is#

  • A personal study and revision notebook for Modern Indian History, built only from the material you provided: the Sarrthi IAS handout chapters (the "book") and the class slides with the teacher's board notes.
  • Nothing is added from outside. Where the teacher's slide and the handout differ, the notebook says so in a note box instead of quietly choosing one.
  • Each chapter follows the same pattern: sections in the order of the class, the teacher's ink in teacher boxes, traps in alert boxes, then a mind map, timeline, flow charts, tables, connections, recall sheet, quiz and flash cards.
Teacher's board

Source for this course = only the Sarrthi IAS handout. After the batch is over, add the new NCERTs (from the Sarrthi IAS Foundation YouTube channel).

2 · The box colours#

Teacher's board

Saffron box: what the teacher wrote or said on the board or slide.

Handout alert

Red box: a trap, a Prelims alert or a common mistake.

Note

Blue box: a note, a clarification, or a difference between the handout and the slides.

Remember

Green box: a takeaway to memorise.

3 · The teacher's study method#

  1. Keep total sync between the uploaded chapters and the PPTs after class.
  2. Reading before class: read the chapters without making gists. Reading after class: make gists.
  3. Make gists in the side margins of the chapter; the second option is the PPT margins.
  4. Do fast reading without highlighting.
  5. MMP (the expansion is not clear on the slides) is another reading of the gists and PPTs, used to make flowcharts of Mains PYQs.
  6. Prelims short notes = gists plus the PPT.
  7. Each chapter has PYQs/FYQs (Prelims 1995-2026, Mains 2013-26).
Note

This notebook's recall sheet and flash cards at the end of each chapter are built for step 2 (quick gists) and step 6 (Prelims short notes).

4 · Priority and PYQ map for all 47 chapters#

  • The teacher's marking is taken from his class slides. The PYQ years are the ones quoted inside each handout chapter (Prelims alerts and Mains alerts) and on the class slides. The handout states that every chapter carries PYQs/FYQs (Prelims 1995-2026, Mains 2013-26); only the ones quoted in the text are listed here. The years were picked up by a text search of the handout, so one year can stand for more than one question, and a year quoted only in passing may be included.
  • Chapters 1 to 8 are covered in this notebook now; each has an Exam lens section at its start with the full question, the answer or trap and where to find it in the chapter.
  • Where the PYQs cluster: the most PYQ years are quoted in Chapter 37 (17), Chapter 41 (15), Chapter 29 (14), Chapter 19 (13), Chapter 42 (11), Chapter 17 (10), Chapter 27 (9), Chapter 46 (9), Chapter 43 (8), Chapter 44 (8) (counts are years quoted, not separate questions). Chapters 2 to 8 quote only 10 PYQ years between them, which matches the teacher's marking of those chapters as the lighter block, with Chapter 8 the exception he calls very important.
  • Mains versus Prelims: the handout quotes Mains PYQs in only a few chapters (2, 4, 6 and 14, 15, 19, 36, 47); almost all quoted PYQs are Prelims. Use the Mains alerts as answer-writing practice and the Prelims alerts as trap lists.
