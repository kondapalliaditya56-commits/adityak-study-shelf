In simple words, key terms and memory words#

In simple words

  • The original Constitution had no authoritative Hindi text. The 58th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1987 fixed this.
  • It inserted Article 394-A in the last part, Part XXII.
  • The President must publish a Hindi translation of the Constitution, matching the language, style and terms of Central Acts in Hindi, with all amendments up to then.
  • The President must also publish a Hindi translation of every later amendment made in English.
  • The Hindi version is read as having the same meaning as the English original, and it is the authoritative Hindi text for all legal purposes.

1 · The 58th Amendment and 394-A#

  • The Constitution originally had no provision for an authoritative text in Hindi. This was addressed later to ensure legal clarity.

Amendment at a glance

ItemDetail
Amendment58th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1987
New Article394-A (a new Article)
Placed inThe last part of the Constitution (Part XXII)
SubjectAuthoritative Hindi text

What 394-A requires

  • The President must publish under his authority:
  • A translation of the Constitution in Hindi.
  • – It must conform to the language, style and terminology used in the authoritative texts of Central Acts in Hindi.
  • – It must incorporate all amendments made up to the date of publication.
  • A Hindi translation of every amendment to the Constitution made in English.

Hindi text procedure

  • President publishes: Under his authority, under 394-A
  • Hindi translation of the Constitution:
    • Matches Central Acts' Hindi
    • Includes all amendments up to publication
  • Hindi translation of each later English amendment: Published for every amendment made in English
  • If difficulty arises: President causes the Hindi text to be revised suitably
Hindi text procedure

Legal standing

  • Equivalence: the translation is construed to have the same meaning as the original English text.
  • Authoritative text: the translated version is deemed the authoritative text in Hindi for all legal purposes.
  • Memory aid (394-A facts): Amendment 58th (1987), Part XXII, Hindi translation by the President, Equals English in meaning: "A Poet Has Ease."
  • Shortcut: Remember. 58, 394-A, XXII: 58th Amendment inserted 394-A in Part XXII (last part).

Original versus translation

  • English original:
    • Source text
    • No provision for an authoritative Hindi text at first
  • Hindi text under 394-A:
    • Same meaning as English
    • Authoritative in Hindi for all legal purposes
    • Revised by the President if difficulty arises

Traps#

Trap 1 · Who publishes and who revises Both publishing the Hindi translation and revising it when difficulty arises are done by the President, not Parliament. Rule: President publishes and revises.
Trap 2 · Amendment number and year The provision came from the 58th Amendment (1987), not from the original 1950 text. Rule: 58 and 1987; the Constitution started with English only.
