In simple words, key terms and memory words#

In simple words

  • Every State has a State Public Service Commission (SPSC), the state twin of the UPSC, under the same Articles 315-323. It is a constitutional body.
  • The Governor appoints the Chairman and members and fixes their service conditions. Term is 6 years or age 62, whichever is earlier (UPSC is 65). At least half must have held office for 10 years under the Union or a State.
  • Only the President removes them, on the same grounds as UPSC members (Art. 317). For misbehaviour the Supreme Court inquires and its advice is binding
  • The Governor can suspend during the inquiry
  • Safeguards: security of tenure, no worsening of service conditions, expenses charged on the State Consolidated Fund, bars on later jobs, and no second term.
  • Functions mirror the UPSC: exams for State services and advice on recruitment, promotions, transfers, discipline, legal costs and injury pensions. Consultation is directory, and advice is not binding.
  • A Joint SPSC is statutory (made by Parliament on request of State Legislatures)
  • The President appoints its members, who also retire at 62

Key terms

SPSC
State Public Service Commission: recruits for State services; constitutional body under Arts. 315-323
Joint SPSC (JSPSC)
Commission for two or more States, created by an Act of Parliament; statutory, not constitutional
Acting Chairman
A member whom the Governor appoints when the Chairman's post is vacant or he cannot work
Consolidated Fund of the State
Source of all SPSC salaries, allowances and pensions (Art. 322); not voted by the Legislature
Directory consultation
Skipping SPSC consultation does not invalidate the government's decision
Exemption regulations
Governor's rules excluding posts from SPSC consultation; laid before the Legislature for 14 days
State Vigilance Commission
Appeared in 1964; also consulted in discipline, but the SPSC has precedence as a constitutional body
Government of India Act, 1935
Envisaged Federal, Provincial and Joint Public Service Commissions; the 1919 Act led to the Central PSC of 1926
Remember it as

Governor appoints, President removes, Governor suspends; 6 years or 62; Joint SPSC is statutory.

1 · Overview and Constitutional Basis#

  • Every state has its own State Public Service Commission (SPSC), the state-level counterpart of the Union Public Service Commission. It is a constitutional body created to secure impartial and professional recruitment for state services.
  • The framework for existence, membership, tenure, safeguards and autonomy is the same as for the UPSC: Part XIV, Articles 315-323.
  • This parity gives state commissions a protected status like the Union body, while they work inside the federal structure.
  • The Constitution sets out: composition, term and conditions of members, grounds and procedure for removal or suspension, power to frame regulations on service conditions, limits on post-service employment, functions, and reporting and financial safeguards.
  • Core idea: SPSC = UPSC framework applied at state level. Same Articles (315-323), same removal grounds, same Article 317 procedure.

2 · Composition and Tenure#

  • An SPSC has a Chairman and a number of Members, all appointed by the Governor.

Composition and tenure at a glance

PointRule
Appointing authorityGovernor of the State
Numerical strengthNot fixed by the Constitution; the Governor decides
QualificationsNo educational or professional qualification prescribed
Experience safeguardOne-half of the Members must have held office for at least ten years under the Government of India or the Government of any State
Conditions of serviceDetermined by the Governor
TermSix years or until age 62, whichever is earlier
UPSC contrastUPSC age limit is 65 years
ResignationAt any time, to the Governor
  • Shortcut: Number trick. 6 and 62 for the state, 65 for the Union. State = smaller number.

Acting Chairman

  • If the Chairman's office is vacant, or he is temporarily unable to perform duties (for example absence or incapacity), the Governor may appoint one of the sitting Members to act as Chairman.
  • The acting Chairman continues until a substantive Chairman assumes office or the original Chairman resumes duties.
  • Memory aid (Composition facts): Governor appoints, Number not fixed, Half the Members with 10 years' service, Six years or 62: "Good Newspapers Have Stories".

Also in the handout

  • They may, however, resign at any time by tendering their resignation to the Governor.

3 · Removal of Members#

  • The appointing authority and the removing authority are different. The Governor appoints, but the President alone can remove the Chairman or a Member. Grounds and procedure are the same as for the UPSC and are governed by Article 317.

Governor versus President

  • Governor:
    • Appoints Chairman and Members
    • Fixes their conditions of service
    • Accepts resignation
    • May appoint an acting Chairman
    • May suspend a Chairman or Member during the Supreme Court enquiry
  • President:
    • Alone can remove
    • Refers misbehaviour charges to the Supreme Court
    • Removes on grounds (a) to (c) below
    • Bound by the Supreme Court's advice
Governor and President in SPSC service
GAP
  • GGovernor suspends during the Supreme Court inquiry
  • AAppointed by the Governor
  • PPresident alone removes

A deliberate GAP between the appointer (Governor) and the remover (President) protects independence. The order is rearranged for memory; the handout does not fix an order. (Memory aid, not from the handout.)

Grounds for removal by the President

Two routes to removal

RouteWhat happens
Express grounds(a) adjudged insolvent; (b) accepts paid employment outside the duties of the office during tenure; (c) in the President's opinion unfit to continue because of mental or physical infirmity
MisbehaviourPresident refers the charge to the Supreme Court for enquiry; removal follows only if the Supreme Court upholds the charge and advises removal; the advice is binding on the President

Removal for misbehaviour

  • Charge of misbehaviour: allegation against Chairman or Member
  • President refers to the Supreme Court: enquiry is judicially supervised
  • Governor may suspend: the member is suspended while the Court inquires, pending the President's final decision
  • Supreme Court reports: upholds the charge and advises removal, or does not
  • President removes:
    • Only after the Court's advice
    • The advice binds the President
Removal for misbehaviour

What counts as misbehaviour

  • A member is guilty of misbehaviour if he or she is concerned or interested in any contract or agreement made by the Government of India or a State Government, or derives profit or benefit from such a contract or agreement. Holding shares as one shareholder among others in an incorporated company is the exception. This narrow definition prevents conflicts of interest.
  • Memory aid (Grounds for removal): Insolvent, Paid job outside office, Infirmity (mental or physical): "It Pays Inside".
  • Shortcut: Who does what. Governor: appoint, suspend. President: remove. Supreme Court: inquire.

  • Six safeguards protect the Commission, as listed by the handout.

5 · Functions of the SPSC#

  • Under Articles 315-323 the SPSC ensures a merit-based, transparent and professional civil service at the state level. Its mandate mirrors the UPSC but for state services.

Primary function

  • Conduct examinations for recruitment to State civil services and posts, giving equal opportunity and competitive selection.

Advisory role

  • The SPSC advises the State executive on personnel matters. Consultation is needed in these areas.

Matters on which the SPSC is consulted

AreaItems
Recruitment and service managementMethod and procedure of recruitment; principles for appointments, promotions and inter-service transfers; suitability of persons for direct recruitment, promotions and transfers, and appointments through transfer or deputation (departmental promotion proposals are referred to the Commission for approval)
Disciplinary mattersCensure (formal reprimand); withholding of increments or promotions; recovery for pecuniary loss caused to the government; demotion to a lower rank or service; compulsory retirement; removal or dismissal from service
Service-related claimsReimbursement of legal expenses for officials defending acts done in the course of duty; grant and quantum of pension or compensation for injuries sustained during service
Other personnel mattersAny additional service administration issue the State executive refers to it
State Judicial ServicesGovernor consults the SPSC (and the High Court) on rules for appointments to State Judicial Services, excluding District Judges
  • Memory aid (Six disciplinary penalties): Censure, Withholding increments or promotion, Recovery of loss, Demotion, Compulsory retirement, Removal or dismissal: "Clerks Who Report Delays Come Right back".

Judicial position on consultation

  • Supreme Court position:
    • Non-consultation or irregular consultation does not invalidate the government's final decision. Consultation is directory, not mandatory. An SPSC recommendation gives no legal right to appointment
    • Selections must still be acted on fairly and without arbitrariness

Extension of functions

  • The State Legislature may expand the SPSC's role by assigning additional functions relating to State services, and by bringing the services of local authorities, public bodies or statutory institutions under SPSC oversight.
  • Such extension must be made through a State law (Article 321).

Reporting to the State Legislature

Annual report

  • SPSC submits report: each year, to the Governor
  • Governor lays it: before both Houses of the State Legislature

6 · Institutional Role within State Administration#

  • The Constitution treats the SPSC as a guardian of meritocracy in state public services.
  • Its advisory scope covers recruitment and evaluation such as promotions and disciplinary proceedings.
  • It does not deal with broader personnel administration such as service classification, pay structures, cadre policies or training. These are handled by the Department of Personnel or the General Administration Department.
  • Its recommendations are advisory
  • The state government may depart from them with legislative accountability
  • The executive can frame rules defining the extent and manner of the Commission's advisory involvement.

Overlap with the State Vigilance Commission

  • The State Vigilance Commission (SVC) emerged in 1964.
  • Both bodies are consulted in disciplinary cases, so overlapping jurisdiction can lead to differing recommendations.
  • As a constitutional authority, the SPSC retains precedence when conflicts arise.

Advisory versus binding

  • What the SPSC does:
    • Conducts exams
    • Advises on recruitment, promotions, discipline and claims
    • Reports annually
  • What it does not do:
    • Bind the government
    • Decide service classification, pay structures, cadre policies or training
    • Give a right to appointment

Also in the handout

  • This procedural requirement ensures that any narrowing of the SPSC’s consultative scope is subject to legislative scrutiny and correction.

7 · Limits on the SPSC's Authority#

  • The Constitution carves out areas where the SPSC is not consulted.

Exclusions from consultation

Excluded areaWho decides
Reservation in favour of any backward class of citizensState executive and legislature
Claims of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in appointments to services and postsState executive and legislature
Posts, services or matters excluded by the Governor through regulationsGovernor, subject to Legislature

Governor's regulations

  • The Governor may exclude specified posts, services or matters from the SPSC's purview by making regulations.
  • Every such regulation must be laid before each House of the State Legislature for at least fourteen days.
  • The State Legislature may amend or repeal those regulations.
  • Memory aid (Exclusions): Reservation for backward classes, SC/ST claims, Governor's regulations: "Rules Set Gently".

8 · Joint State Public Service Commission#

  • Two or more States may have a Joint State Public Service Commission (JSPSC). It is created by a law of Parliament when the Legislatures of the States pass resolutions requesting it (Article 315).

SPSC versus JSPSC

PointSPSCJSPSC
NatureConstitutional bodyStatutory body (created only by a Parliament law on state request)
Appointing authorityGovernorPresident
TenureSix years or age 62, whichever earlierSix years or age 62, whichever earlier
Suspension and removalPresident removes; Governor may suspend during SC enquiryPresident suspends or removes
ResignationTo the GovernorTo the President
Number of members and conditions of serviceGovernor decidesPresident decides under the enabling statute
Annual reportTo the GovernorTo each of the Governors of the States it serves; each places it before his State Legislature

Other points

  • Where a single State needs support, the UPSC may serve the State on the Governor's request and with Presidential approval, an alternate statutory route for cooperation in recruitment.
  • Historical roots: Under the Government of India Act, 1919, a Central Public Service Commission was constituted (1926). The Government of India Act, 1935 envisaged a Federal Public Service Commission and also Provincial and Joint Public Service Commissions for groups of provinces. These precedents inform the modern JSPSC.
Handout alert

Handout alert: Do not carry the SPSC rule over to the JSPSC. For an SPSC the Governor appoints and the President removes. For a JSPSC the President both appoints and removes (the handout says so for both).

Traps#

Trap 1 · Appointer is not the remover The Governor appoints SPSC members, but only the President can remove them. The Governor can only suspend while the Supreme Court inquires. Rule: Governor = appoint and suspend; President = remove.
Trap 2 · Age 62 for state, 65 for Union SPSC and Joint SPSC members serve six years or until 62; UPSC members until 65. Rule: only the UPSC has 65.
Trap 3 · Joint SPSC is not constitutional UPSC and SPSC are created directly by the Constitution; a Joint SPSC exists only by a law of Parliament on the request of the States, and the President appoints its members. Rule: Joint SPSC = statutory, President appoints, report goes to every Governor concerned.
Trap 4 · What the SPSC is not consulted on Backward-class reservations and SC/ST claims are outside consultation. The Governor can exclude more posts by regulations laid for 14 days. Unlike the UPSC list, no tribunal, diplomatic or Group C/D exclusion is stated here. Rule: two core exclusions plus whatever the Governor excludes.
