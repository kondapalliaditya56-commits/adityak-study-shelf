In simple words, key terms and memory words#

In simple words

  • The UPSC is the main central recruiting agency, created directly by the Constitution in Part XIV (Articles 315-323).
  • The President appoints the Chairman and members. Term is 6 years or age 65, whichever is earlier
  • at least half must have held government service for 10 years. A former member cannot be reappointed
  • Only the President removes them. For misbehaviour the matter goes to the Supreme Court for inquiry. For insolvency, outside paid job, or infirmity, no SC reference is needed.
  • Independence: non-diminution of service conditions (Art. 318), expenses charged on the Consolidated Fund (Art. 322), and bars on later government jobs (Art. 319).
  • It holds exams for All India Services, Central Services and UT services, and is consulted on recruitment methods, promotions, transfers and discipline. Its advice is advisory, not binding
  • Consultation is directory, not mandatory
  • It is the recruiting agency
  • DoPT is the personnel agency. Annual report goes to the President, with a memorandum on advice not accepted

1 · Overview: a constitutional organ#

  • The Union Public Service Commission is the principal central recruiting agency of India. It is not a statutory or executive creation but a constitutional organ, established directly by the Constitution for an impartial and authoritative process of selecting personnel for central services.
  • Framework: Part XIV (Articles 315-323).
  • Provisions cover composition, appointment and removal of members and safeguards that secure independence.
  • Memory aid (Articles 315-323 in order): Establishment (315), Appointment and tenure (316), Removal and suspension (317), Conditions of service (318), Post-tenure employment bar (319), Functions (320), Additional functions (321), Expenses (322), Report (323): "Every Aspirant Reads Constitution, Preparing For Admission, Expecting Results."

2 · Article 315: Public Service Commissions for the Union and the States#

  • A PSC shall exist for the Union and for each State.
  • Two or more States may, by resolutions of their Legislatures, request Parliament to establish a Joint State Public Service Commission for common needs.
  • The Parliamentary law may include incidental or consequential provisions.
  • The UPSC, if requested by a State Governor and approved by the President, may assist a State PSC in recruitment.
  • A reference to UPSC or SPSC in the Constitution means whichever Commission serves the relevant government's need in the matter concerned.
  • Handout illustration: Sikkim in 1975 sought UPSC assistance for recruitment and the request was accepted. A similar request by Punjab in 2008 was not approved.

UPSC helping a State

  • State Governor requests: Asks UPSC to assist in recruitment
  • President approves: Approval required
  • UPSC assists: Serves the State's recruitment need
UPSC helping a State
  • Shortcut: Who creates what. Union and each State: Constitution. Joint PSC: Parliament law on request of State Legislatures. UPSC help to a State: Governor asks, President approves.

3 · Article 316: Appointment and tenure of members#

CommissionAppointing authority
UPSCPresident
Joint State PSCPresident
State PSC (SPSC)Governor of the State
  • At least half of the Commission's members must have held government service for a minimum of ten years under the Union or a State government.
  • If the Chairman's office is vacant or the Chairman cannot perform functions, the President (UPSC or Joint) or Governor (SPSC) may authorise one of the members to act as Chairperson temporarily.

Tenure

  • Each member serves six years from the date of entering office, or until the age limit, whichever is earlier.

Age limits

  • UPSC: 65 years
  • SPSC or Joint Commission: 62 years
  • A member resigns by written letter to the President (UPSC or Joint) or to the Governor (State Commission).
  • A former member cannot be reappointed to the Commission. But a serving member may be elevated to Chairperson, giving a fresh term within the age limits.
  • Memory aid (Article 316 numbers): Six years, Half the members with ten years of service, Two ages (65 and 62): "Six Hens Travel."
  • Shortcut: Tenure in one line. 6 years or age limit, whichever first. 65 for UPSC, 62 for SPSC and Joint PSC. No reappointment, but a member can become Chairperson.

4 · Article 317: Removal and suspension#

  • Only the President can remove the Chairman or a member of a PSC.
  • For misbehaviour, the matter must be referred to the Supreme Court for inquiry. If the Court upholds the charges, the President may order removal.
  • Pending the decision, the President (or the Governor for an SPSC) may suspend the member.

Removal for misbehaviour

  • Misbehaviour alleged: Matter referred to the Supreme Court
  • Supreme Court inquiry: Charges examined
  • Charges upheld: Court finds them proved
  • President removes: Order of removal
Removal for misbehaviour

Removal without Supreme Court reference

  • The President may remove a member if the member:
  • is adjudged insolvent;
  • Engages in paid employment outside Commission duties
  • Or
  • is deemed unfit due to infirmity of mind or body.
  • Contract rule: A member having an interest in a government contract, or enjoying benefits from such a contract, is guilty of misbehaviour under this Article.
  • Memory aid (No-reference grounds): Insolvent, paid Employment outside, Infirmity of mind or body: "In Every Institution."
  • Shortcut: Misbehaviour versus the three. Misbehaviour = SC inquiry first. Insolvency, outside paid job, infirmity = President removes directly. Suspension pending inquiry: President or Governor.

5 · Articles 318 and 319: service conditions and post-tenure bars#

  • The President (UPSC or Joint) or the Governor (SPSC) may by regulations fix the conditions of service, number of members and staffing of the Commission.
  • Service conditions cannot be altered to a member's disadvantage after appointment.

Article 319: employment after leaving office

  • Strict limits protect impartiality:

  • Memory aid (Article 318 and 319): Non-diminution of service conditions (318) and Cooling bar on later jobs (319): "No Change, No Cash-in."

6 · Article 320: duties and consultation#

  • The handout describes the constitutional pattern first for the State PSC: its primary responsibility is to conduct examinations for appointments to State civil services and posts. These duties under clauses (1) and (2) are mandatory.

The State Government must consult the SPSC on

  • methods of recruitment;
  • principles of appointment, promotion and transfer;
  • suitability of candidates for such actions;
  • disciplinary matters of State civil servants;
  • claims for reimbursement of legal expenses from official duties;
  • pension claims for injuries on service.
  • Flexibility proviso: The Governor may issue regulations saying when consultation is not necessary. These regulations must be laid before the State Legislature for not less than 14 days and can be modified in the same session.

7 · Articles 321, 322 and 323#

Article 321: additional functions

  • Parliament or a State Legislature may by law give the UPSC or SPSC additional functions relating to services of the Union, State, local authorities, statutory bodies or public institutions.

Article 322: financial independence

  • All expenses of a Union or State PSC, including salaries, allowances and pensions, are charged on the Consolidated Fund of India or of the State. Charged expenditure is not subject to vote, so the Commission's financial autonomy is protected.

Article 323: annual report

8 · Core functions of the UPSC#

  • A multidimensional mandate: recruitment, advisory supervision and personnel governance for the Union and certain centrally administered services.

Recruitment and examinations

  • Competitive examinations for All India Services, Central Services, and Services of Union Territories and other centrally administered regions. Includes written tests, interviews and all selection procedures.

Help to States

  • Joint recruitment schemes: UPSC may assist two or more States that request cooperation for joint recruitment or need candidates with specialised qualifications.
  • Individual States: UPSC may serve a State in recruitment or advisory matters only when the Governor requests and the President approves.

Advisory role: matters on which the Union Government must consult UPSC

Nine consultation matters

No.Matter

2Principles of appointments, promotions and transfers across services

4Disciplinary cases: censure; withholding of increments or promotions; recovery of financial loss; demotion; compulsory retirement; removal or dismissal

  • These consultations establish procedural fairness across service conditions.

Nature of consultation (as clarified by the Supreme Court)

  • Consultation with UPSC is directory, not mandatory.
  • Government decisions do not automatically become invalid if consultation is omitted.
  • UPSC selection gives no right to appointment.
  • Yet the Government must act fairly, avoiding arbitrariness or mala fides.

Expansion of jurisdiction

  • Parliament may confer additional functions on the UPSC and place the personnel systems of public authorities or corporate bodies under UPSC jurisdiction.

Annual report to Parliament

  • UPSC submits its report to the President, who lays it before both Houses of Parliament.
  • A memorandum must detail every instance where UPSC advice was not accepted.
  • Such non-acceptance must be approved by the Appointments Committee of the Union Cabinet (ACUC), not by a single ministry or department.
  • Memory aid (Disciplinary penalties): Censure, Withholding increments or promotions, Recovery of loss, Demotion, Compulsory retirement, Removal or dismissal: "Cool Winds Rush Down Cool Rooms."
  • Memory aid (Nine consultation heads): Methods, Principles, Suitability, Disciplinary, Legal expenses, Pension, Temporary posts over one year, Extension or re-employment, Other referred matters: "My Policy Stays Direct, Lightly Pressing To End Objections."

9 · Constitutional limits on UPSC's consultative role#

  • The Constitution limits the consultative role in a few areas to preserve executive discretion in social justice policy and urgent staffing.

Temporary staffingTemporary or officiating posts where tenure is not expected to exceed one year
Presidential power to expand exclusionsThe President may by regulations remove more posts or matters from UPSC oversight for All-India and Central Services. Regulations are laid before Parliament for 14 days and are open to amendment or repeal

10 · UPSC as guardian of merit: scope and limits#

  • The Constitution envisages the UPSC as the watch-dog of the merit system.
  • It handles recruitment to All-India Services and Central Services (Group A and B) under Article 320 and advises on promotions and disciplinary matters of central civil servants when consulted.

UPSC versus DoPT

  • UPSC:
    • Central recruiting authority
    • Conducts examinations and selections
    • Advises when consulted
  • DoPT:
    • Central personnel agency, within the Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions
    • Service classification, pay scales, cadre management, training and wider personnel policy

Advice is advisory

  • UPSC advice is non-binding: the Union Government may accept or reject it.
  • The safeguard is accountability to Parliament: any departure must be explained in the President's annual report.
  • The Government may frame rules that define or limit the scope of the UPSC's advisory role.

UPSC and the CVC

  • The Central Vigilance Commission (CVC) emerged in 1964, and the Government also consults it in disciplinary proceedings.
  • Conflicting advice can cause tension, but UPSC keeps a constitutional advantage: it is a constitutional body, whereas the CVC began by executive decision and got statutory status in October 2003.
  • Shortcut: UPSC versus DoPT. UPSC recruits. DoPT manages pay, cadre, training and policy. UPSC does not do classification, pay scales, cadre, training or policy.
  • Memory aid (UPSC does not do): Classification, Pay scales, Cadre management, Training, Personnel policy: "Cats Prefer Cream, Tuna, Prawns." These belong to the DoPT.

11 · ARC-1 recommendations and the Key Articles table#

ARC-1 recommendations on the working of Public Service Commissions

  • One member of an SPSC should come from outside the state.
  • Members should possess a graduation degree.
  • The Chairman of UPSC and of the SPSC should be consulted while appointing SPSC members.
  • The Chairman of UPSC should be consulted while appointing UPSC members and his successor.
  • Two-thirds of the members of UPSC should be drawn from among Chairmen of SPSCs.
  • Qualifications for official members and unofficial members should be set out.
  • Memory aid (ARC-1 six): Outside member in SPSC, Graduation, Consult chairmen for SPSC members, Consult UPSC chairman for UPSC members and successor, Two-thirds of UPSC from SPSC chairmen, Qualifications of official and unofficial members: "Old Governments Choose Careful Teams, Quietly."

  • The President decides it. No qualification is prescribed except that half the members must have held office 10 years under the Union or a State

Traps#

Trap 1 · 65 versus 62 UPSC members retire at 65; a State PSC or Joint Commission member retires at 62. Both have a six-year term, whichever comes first. Rule: UPSC = 65, SPSC and Joint = 62.
Trap 2 · When the Supreme Court is needed Only removal for misbehaviour needs an SC inquiry. Insolvency, outside paid work and unfitness of mind or body let the President remove without it. Rule: misbehaviour = SC reference; the other three = direct.
Trap 3 · Mandatory or directory The Supreme Court held consultation with the UPSC is directory. Skipping it does not void a decision, and a UPSC selection gives no right to appointment. Rule: advice is non-binding; the Government must still act fairly, without mala fides.
Trap 4 · Who the UPSC recruits for UPSC recruits for All India Services, Central Services (Groups A and B mainly) and UT services. Most Group C and D posts, tribunals and diplomatic heads are outside its consultation. Rule: reservations, SC/ST claims, short temporary posts, tribunals, diplomatic heads, Group C/D = no UPSC consultation.

Book edition: simple words and memory words#

In simple words

  • The UPSC is the main central recruiting agency, created directly by the Constitution in Part XIV (Articles 315-323).
  • The President appoints the Chairman and members. Term is 6 years or age 65, whichever is earlier; at least half must have held government service for 10 years. A former member cannot be reappointed.
  • Only the President removes them. For misbehaviour the matter goes to the Supreme Court for inquiry. For insolvency, outside paid job, or infirmity, no SC reference is needed.
  • Independence: non-diminution of service conditions (Art. 318), expenses charged on the Consolidated Fund (Art. 322), and bars on later government jobs (Art. 319).
  • It holds exams for All India Services, Central Services and UT services, and is consulted on recruitment methods, promotions, transfers and discipline. Its advice is advisory, not binding; consultation is directory, not mandatory.
  • It is the recruiting agency; DoPT is the personnel agency. Annual report goes to the President, with a memorandum on advice not accepted.

Key terms

Art. 315
A Public Service Commission for the Union and each State; Joint State PSC possible by Parliament's law
Art. 317
Removal and suspension; misbehaviour needs a Supreme Court inquiry
Art. 320
Functions: exams, and consultation on recruitment, promotions, discipline, legal costs, injury pensions
Art. 322
Expenses charged on the Consolidated Fund, so not voted upon
Directory (not mandatory)
Failure to consult UPSC does not invalidate a government decision; selection gives no right to a post
ACUC
Appointments Committee of the Union Cabinet; must approve any non-acceptance of UPSC advice
DoPT
Department of Personnel and Training: central personnel agency (pay, cadre, training, policy)
CVC
Central Vigilance Commission (1964, statutory Oct 2003) also consulted in discipline; UPSC has the edge as a constitutional body
