1 · What this notebook is#

Two Sarrthi IAS GS-2 Governance modules, rebuilt as one notebook you can read on the tablet: Chapter 1 covers Module 1 (sections 1-14, civil services to RTI) and Chapter 2 covers Module 2 (sections 15-19, corruption to the Prevention of Corruption Act).

Every section opens with an Exam lens (priority, PYQs quoted, Prelims and Mains hooks). Page references such as M1 p.5 are the printed page numbers of each module. Only facts printed in the handouts are used. No class slides or teacher board notes were supplied, so there is no teacher-ink forecast; priority comes from where the handouts quote PYQs.

Source conflict

Module 1 says India appointed a central ombudsman in 2013; Module 2 says the Lokpal was constituted in 2019. The notes use “Act 2013, constituted 2019”.

Counts

19 Mains PYQ entries quoted (2013-2024) and no Prelims PYQ. Priority rule: High = 2 or more PYQs map to the section; Medium = 1 PYQ or a partial fit (*); Low = none quoted.

Blank-page prompts#

  • Write the six roles of civil services and one sentence on each.
  • List the Articles 309 to 323A and 16 with one line each.
  • Draw the Sec 6A chain from the Single Directive to Sec 17A.
  • List seven problems of the Indian Citizen's Charter programme and a fix for each.

Limits of the material#

  • The 2014, 2017 and 2020 questions in Module 1 are printed without marks; 2017 and 2020 give word limits (250 and 150). Rule: plan the 2014-type answer at 10-15 marks from the notes, check the paper's marks on the day.
  • Public Examination Act 2024 (M2 p.20), Satyam 2009 (p.21) and DBT 2022 (one line only) are not covered; the CBI 2021 question needs state-consent material not in the notes. Rule: cover them from other material.
