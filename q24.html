1998PYQ 1(Prelims 1998)

The Congress is tottering to its fall and one of my great ambitions while in India, is to assist it to a peaceful demise. This statement is attributed to

Answer: (B)

(B) ✓ — The statement is Lord Curzon's, from his viceroyalty (1899–1905): he saw the Congress as "tottering to its fall" and hoped to assist its "peaceful demise" — a prediction the INC's later history proved spectacularly wrong.

Wrong options:

(A) ✗ — Dufferin84-88 belongs to the INC's FOUNDING story — Hume met Viceroy Dufferin and the two brought educated Indians together in 1885. A founding-era patron of 1885 is not the author of the 1900 demise prophecy.

(C) ✗ — Lytton76-80 left India in 1880 — five years BEFORE the INC was born (Bombay, December 1885). He could not have spoken of assisting the demise of an organisation that did not yet exist.

(D) ✗ — "None of these" fails because the attribution is settled: the words are Curzon's (Viceroy 1899–1905), not an anonymous remark.

1998PYQ 2(Prelims 1998)

Which of the following pairs are correctly matched? List I: 1. Theodore Beck 2. Ilbert Bill 3. Pherozeshah Mehta 4. Badruddin Tyabji List II: 1. Mohammadan Anglo Oriental College, Aligarh 2. Lord Ripon 3. Indian National Congress. 4. Muslim League Select the correct answer using the codes below:

Answer: (D)

Pair 4 is wrong: Badruddin Tyabji was the 1st Muslim INC President (Madras Session 1887) and died in August 1906 — BEFORE the Muslim League was founded (December 1906, Dacca).

Wrong options:

(A) ✗ — Overcounts: pair 4 fails — Tyabji died in August 1906, months before the Muslim League's founding at Dacca (December 1906); his place in history is as the 1st Muslim INC President (Madras 1887).

(B) ✗ — Keeps the one wrong pair (4) and throws away two right ones: Beck → MAO College Aligarh and Mehta → INC are both correctly matched.

(C) ✗ — Drops the correct pair 2 — the Ilbert Bill belongs to Ripon's viceroyalty, 1883 — while keeping the wrong pair 4. (The single trap in this question is pair 4: Tyabji belonged to the INC, not the Muslim League — he died before the League even existed.) (Note: Theodore Beck - First Principal of Mohammeden Anglo oriented College, Aligarh | Lord Ripon - Ilbert bill was passed in 1883 | Pherozshah Mehta - One of the founder members of Indian National Congress.)

2000PYQ 3(Prelims 2000)

Consider the following statements about the Indian National Congress 1. Sarojini Naidu was the first woman to be the President of the Congress. 2. CR Das was in prison when he functioned as the President of the Congress. 3. The first Britisher to become the President of the Congress was Alan Octavian (AO) Hume. 4. Alfred Webb was the President of the Congress in 1894. Which of these statements are correct?

Answer: (B)

(B) ✓ — Statements 2 and 4 are correct. Statement 1 (Naidu first woman President) is WRONG — Annie Besant was first woman INC President at Calcutta Session 1917. Statement 2 (C.R. Das as President while in prison) is CORRECT — Das presided over Gaya Session 1922 from prison. Statement 3 (A.O. Hume first Britisher INC President) is WRONG — Hume was Founder/General Secretary, never President; George Yule was first Britisher INC President at Allahabad Session 1888. Statement 4 (Alfred Webb 1894) is CORRECT. Therefore answer is

(B) 2 and 4.

Wrong options:

(A) ✗ — Both its statements are the false ones: the first woman INC President was Annie Besant (Calcutta 1917), not Sarojini Naidu (Kanpur 1925, the second); and A.O. Hume never presided — the 1st British President was George Yule (Allahabad 1888).

(C) ✗ — Statement 3 contaminates it: Hume served as the INC's General Secretary and never held the presidency; the first Britisher in the chair was George Yule (1888).

(D) ✗ — Includes both false statements: 1 (Besant, not Naidu, was the first woman President) and 3 (Hume never presided). (Note: Sarojini Naidu was second woman to be the President of Congress, first being Annie Besant in 1917. A O Hume was the founder of Congress, but he never became its President. Hence statement 2 and 4 is correct.)

Answer: (B)

Wrong options:

(C) ✗ — Calcutta is the classic trap: it hosted the SECOND session (1886, under Dadabhai Naoroji), not the first.

2011PYQ 6(Prelims 2011)

What was the purpose which Sir William Wedderburn and WS Caine had set-up the Indian Parliamentary Committee in 1893?

Answer: (A)

(A) ✓ — The Indian Parliamentary Committee (set up by Wedderburn & Caine, 1893) was a pressure group of sympathetic British MPs; its purpose was to raise and press Indian political reforms in the House of Commons.

Wrong options:

(B) ✗ — The Committee's arena was the House of Commons and its object Indian political REFORM; entry of Indians into the imperial judiciary was never its brief — Wedderburn's own record shows the judiciary issue ran the other way: his pro-Indian positions cost him a Bombay High Court judgeship.

(C) ✗ — In 1893 the vocabulary was reform WITHIN British rule, not independence: as an MP, Wedderburn supported INC demands through the 1890s in partnership with Gokhale — demands for representative reform.

(D) ✗ — The Committee was a pressure group OF sympathetic British MPs inside Parliament, not a vehicle for sending eminent Indians INTO Parliament. (Note: The purpose of Sir William Wedderburn and WS Caine was to agitate for Indian political reforms in the House of Commons in 1893.)

2015PYQ 7(Prelims 2015)

Consider the following statements: 1. The first woman President of the Indian National Congress was Sarojini Naidu. 2. The first Muslim President of the Indian National Congress was Badruddin Tyabji. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Answer: (B)

(B) ✓ — Statement 2 is correct: Badruddin Tyabji was the first Muslim President of the INC (Madras Session, 1887). Statement 1 is wrong: the first woman President was Annie Besant (Calcutta, 1917); Sarojini Naidu came second (Kanpur, 1925).

Wrong options:

(A) ✗ — Statement 1 is the false one — Annie Besant (1917), not Sarojini Naidu (1925), was the first woman in the INC chair.

(C) ✗ — "Both 1 and 2" fails on statement 1: Naidu was the SECOND woman President (Kanpur 1925), after Annie Besant (Calcutta 1917).

(D) ✗ — "Neither" fails on statement 2, which is true: Tyabji presided at Madras in 1887 as the first Muslim INC President. (Note: Annie Besant was the first woman President of Indian National Congress. She presided over the 1917 Calcutta session of the Indian National Congress. Annie Besant was of Irish origin.)
