1 · Regions of Oceania and the continent at a glance#

Class slides 151–154

OCEANIA

7.1 Continent at a Glance Oceania is the continent, while Australia is the biggest continental landmass of Oceania.

The United Nations broadly divides Oceania into four sub-regions:

  • Australia and New Zealand (Australasia)
  • Melanesia: Papua New Guinea, Fiji, Solomon Islands, Vanuatu, New Caledonia
  • Micronesia: Kiribati, Marshall Islands, Federated States of Micronesia, Nauru, Palau
  • Polynesia: Samoa, Tonga, Tuvalu, Cook Islands, French Polynesia, etc Hawaii is also considered to be a part of broader Oceania; it belongs to the USA. The Island of New Guinea is also considered a part of Oceania, even though a part of it belongs to Indonesia, which is an Asian Country.
