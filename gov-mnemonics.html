Governance: mnemonics

CRCC

Mnemonic · Pay, 8th CPC and right to strike
FORMULA

Mnemonic · Civil services reforms and Nolan principles · own mnemonic

Nolan's seven: Honesty · Accountability · Leadership · Openness · Integrity · Objectivity · Selflessness. Handout order S-I-O-A-O-H-L.
Mnemonic · Challenges to reform; Karmayogi; Aarambh · own mnemonic

ICPS
Karmayogi: iGOT · Competency · Performance · SPV (+ the Commission).
Mnemonic · Challenges to reform; Karmayogi; Aarambh
CCC
Aarambh (Common Foundation Course): capacity-building initiative nurturing a shared understanding of governance among civil services probationers; stresses three foundational values for new entrants: Commitment · Character · Conduct.
Mnemonic · Transparency and NDSAP · own mnemonic
TOP
Three metaphors: Tool of good governance · Open decision-making (secrecy vs privacy) · Public value (counters corruption).
Mnemonic · Transparency and NDSAP · own mnemonic
ACETIPS
Objectives: Accountability · Combat corruption · Entitlements · Trust · Informed citizens · Participation · Service delivery
Mnemonic · Accountability · own mnemonic
EAR
Components: Enforcement (sanctions for failure) · Answerability (ability to justify an action) · Responsiveness (responding to demands of the organisation and its clients).
Mnemonic · Accountability · own mnemonic
CHAI
Dimensions: Controlling political power (prevent abuse via sanctions, transparency, duty to justify) · Human (ethics, public values, trust in govt) · Argumentative (citizens may seek explanations; authorities must justify conduct) · Informational (citizens may receive information; institutions must give details).
Mnemonic · Citizen's Charter and Sevottam · own mnemonic
WHOCARE
ARC-II fixes: Widest possible consultation · Hold officers accountable for results (penal provisions) · One size does not fit all (separate charter per unit and per service) · Commit only to what you can deliver (fix timelines after assessing resources and workload) · Appropriate redressal mechanism · Reform internal structures and processes to match charter commitments (obsolete rules, power distribution) · Evaluate periodically with end-user feedback
Mnemonic · Citizen's Charter and Sevottam · own mnemonic
DSDPMEI
Seven steps in order: Define services · Set standards · Develop capability · Perform · Monitor · Evaluate impact · Improve. Remember: Do Something Daily; People Must Expect Improvement.
Mnemonic · RTI Act 2005 and the 2019 amendment
MONOPOLY
Monopoly + Discretion − Transparency = Corruption. Adding transparency through RTI checks corruption.
Mnemonic · RTI Act 2005 and the 2019 amendment · own mnemonic
PAID
A sound regime: Penalties for denial (Rs 250 a day up to Rs 25,000, plus option of departmental disciplinary action) · Access wide (minimal fees, free for BPL, easily perceivable information) · Independent appeal (CIC, SIC) · Disclosure maximum, exemption minimal
Mnemonic · RTI Act 2005 and the 2019 amendment
16·120·100·5·10·20
Publish 16 types of information within 120 days of the Act (update yearly) · designate PIOs in all units within 100 days · transfer a misdirected request within 5 days · third-party notice 10 days · exempt info released after 20 years. Reply 30 days. Penalty Rs 250/day, up to Rs 25,000. Also: keep records catalogued, indexed, computerised; give as much info suo motu as possible to cut RTI requests.
Mnemonic · CIC, RTI issues, impact and 2nd ARC
1+10 · 3/65 · 5
1 CIC + up to 10 ICs · term 3 years or age 65, earlier one · no reappointment · IC elevated to CIC: total max 5 years.
Mnemonic · CIC, RTI issues, impact and 2nd ARC · own mnemonic
BICEP
Without SC inquiry: Bankrupt · Infirm · Convicted (moral turpitude) · Employed (paid) · Prejudicial financial interest.

Module 2 · Corruption, Social Audit, CBI, CVC, Lokpal, Whistleblower, PCA#

Mnemonic · Problem of Corruption
SPIES
Corruption SPIES on the nation: Social · Political and legal · International · Economic · Security.
Mnemonic · Problem of Corruption
APPLE
Corruption is the rotten APPLE; five kinds of factors rot it: Administrative · Psycho-social · Political · Legal · Economic.
Mnemonic · Problem of Corruption
HORNETS
Psycho-social stings: History · Opportunity theory · Religious context · No popular sanction · Evils · THYMOS · Squirrel Nuts Syndrome.
Mnemonic · Problem of Corruption
CPI
CPI by Transparency International · annual since 1995 · 0 = most corrupt, 100 = least corrupt. 2020 CPI (released 2021): New Zealand and Denmark least corrupt; Somalia, South Sudan last; South Asia: Bhutan 24th; USA 25th, Botswana 35th, China 78th, Pakistan 124th (as printed).
Mnemonic · Tackling Corruption and Social Audit
SCARI
Stick (prosecution, stings, asset declaration) · Carrot (pay rise with enforcement, helplines) · Administrative and technical (rotation, audit, procurement) · Radical (disband, gender balance, amnesty) · International (FCPA, UK Bribery Act, OECD, UNCAC, FATF).
Mnemonic · Tackling Corruption and Social Audit
MOST
Social audit hurdles: Mainstream policy discourse ignores it · Opposition from vested interests · Statutory in very few programmes (NREGA) · Trained auditors lacking.
Mnemonic · CBI and CVC · own mnemonic
SCATTER
Single Directive struck down · CVC superintends CBI on PCA cases · Appointment of Director by a committee · Tenure minimum 2 years · Transfer by the same committee · Executive (DoPT) sets only the policy framework · Reproach remains: still called a "caged parrot".
Mnemonic · CBI and CVC
1+2 · 4 · 65
1 Commissioner + up to 2 Vigilance Commissioners · term 4 years · age 65 · no reappointment.
Mnemonic · Lokpal in India
9 · 25 · 45 · 5/70
9 members · 25 years' experience · min age 45 · term 5 years or 70.
Mnemonic · Lokpal in India
COPES
Selection committee: CJI or nominated SC judge · Opposition leader (Lok Sabha) · PM (Chair) · Eminent jurist (chosen by the four) · Speaker of Lok Sabha.
Mnemonic · Lokpal in India
PAISE
Act covers the PM only partially: the Lokpal shall not investigate a PM decision on "the Lokpal cannot count the PM's PAISE": Public order · Atomic energy · Internal security · Space · External security and international relations. Even otherwise, the full bench must approve, by a two-thirds majority, the start of an inquiry or investigation against the PM.
Mnemonic · Whistleblower Protection and PCA 1988 · own mnemonic
SAFE
Law Commission + 2nd ARC: Strong law protecting genuine whistleblowers in public and private sectors (extend to corporate whistleblowers exposing fraud or serious harm to public interest) · Anonymity and confidentiality · Fear removed: legal protection against victimisation, administrative safeguards against harassment and bodily harm · Encouragement: SEBI's tipping mechanism pays up to Rs 1 crore for actionable information on insider trading and assures cooperation and confidentiality.
Mnemonic · Whistleblower Protection and PCA 1988 · own mnemonic
BRIBE
Bribe-giving an offence · Report within 7 days (coercive) · Includes retired servants (17A) · Business activities covered · Expedited trial: 2 years, up to 4 · jail term raised from 3 to 7 years.

Mnemonic · E-governance: stages, models, benefits, issues · own mnemonic
BCEI
Four models in handout order: Broadcasting · Critical information flow · E-mobilisation · Interactive service.
Mnemonic · E-governance: stages, models, benefits, issues
6 C's
Teacher's 6 C's of work plan: Content development (revise regularly, relevant to the zone) · Competence building (e.g. bank staff blame "server fault" and wait for an expert: give basic ICT training) · Connectivity, esp. rural (else only the integrated part benefits, majority left on margins) · Citizen's interface (needs phone and internet; internet costly for rural people; awareness; accessible and affordable) · Cyber laws (data protection and security; technology-agnostic; balance privacy so that productive use of technology is not compromised) · Capital (sites need revenue streams; reasonable charges, self-sustaining).
Mnemonic · Data protection, privacy and cybersecurity · own mnemonic
SLIPB
Benefits: Security (national) · Law enforcement access · India's own privacy rules apply · Privacy grievance in Indian courts · Bargaining power.
Mnemonic · Data protection, privacy and cybersecurity · own mnemonic
SHARPDD
Principles: Structured enforcement · Holistic cover (private + govt) · Agnostic technology · Responsible fiduciary · Purpose-limited minimisation · Deterrent penalties · Data consent (informed).
