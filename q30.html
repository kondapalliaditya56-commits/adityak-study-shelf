FYQ 1

Who founded the Anushilan Samiti in Calcutta in 1902?

Answer: (C)

(C)

(C) ✓ — Pramathanath Mitra (P. Mitra) founded the Anushilan Samiti in Calcutta in 1902 as a body for physical training & character building, whose inner purpose was to cultivate the spirit of armed resistance.

Why others wrong: (a) Pulin Behari Das founded the Dacca Anushilan Samiti in 1906, not the Calcutta Samiti of 1902; (b) Barindra Kumar Ghosh was one of the founders of the Jugantar newspaper, not of the Anushilan Samiti; (d) Bhupendranath Datta was a co-founder of the Jugantar newspaper, not of the Anushilan Samiti.

Memory hook: P. Mitra plants the seed (1902), Pulin Das grows the Dacca tree (1906).

FYQ 2

Where did Madame Bhikaji Cama unfurl the first version of the Indian national flag in August 1907?

Answer: (C)

(C)

(C) ✓ — In August 1907, Madame Bhikaji Cama unfurled the first version of the Indian national flag at the International Socialist Congress in Stuttgart, Germany — a dramatic assertion of Indian sovereignty on a global stage, years before any official tricolour was adopted.

Why others wrong: (a) Paris was Madame Cama's operating base for revolutionary work, but the flag was unfurled at the Stuttgart Congress, not in Paris; (b) London hosted India House under Shyamji Krishna Varma & Savarkar, not the flag-unfurling event; (d) Geneva is unconnected with the 1907 flag event.

Memory hook: Stuttgart 1907 — Cama plants India's first flag on foreign soil.

FYQ 3

It set up India House in London as a student hostel that promoted nationalist views. Which of the statements given above are correct?

Answer: (D)

(D)

(D) ✓ — All three are correct. The IHRS was founded in 1905 in London by Shyamji Krishna Varma; only Indians could become members; and it set up India House in London — a student hostel that actively promoted nationalist views among students.

Why others wrong: (a) omits statement 3 which is correct; (b) omits statement 1 which is correct; (c) omits statement 2 which is correct.

Memory hook: IHRS 1905 — London base, Indians only, India House hostel.

FYQ 4

With reference to the Alipore Conspiracy Case of 1908, which one of the following statements is correct?

Answer: (C)

(C)

(C) ✓ — In the Alipore Conspiracy Case (1908), Aurobindo Ghosh was acquitted because B.C. Pal refused to testify; he later escaped to Pondicherry. Barindra Kumar Ghosh & Ullaskar Dutta were found guilty & sentenced to death, later commuted to transportation for life in the Cellular Jail.

Why others wrong: (a) Aurobindo was acquitted, not transported; (b) the positions are reversed — Aurobindo was acquitted, Barindra was sentenced; (d) Barindra was not acquitted — he was transported for life.

Memory hook: Alipore — Aurobindo escapes to Pondicherry, Barindra to Kala Pani.

(D)

FYQ 8

Why was Bal Gangadhar Tilak charged with sedition and deported to Mandalay in 1908?

Answer: (B)

(B)

(B) ✓ — In his newspaper Kesari, Tilak condemned the violence of Prafulla Chaki & Khudiram Bose but defended them on the deeper ground that sustained oppression will produce violence; the article was titled 'Arrival of Bomb.' This nuanced position was enough for the colonial government to charge him with sedition — six years' imprisonment, deported to Mandalay.

Why others wrong: (a) Tilak did not fund the Muzzafarpur bombing; the charge was sedition through his Kesari article; (c) Tilak was not an organiser of the Alipore conspirators; (d) the Surat split (1907) was an INC internal split, not the cause of the sedition trial.

Memory hook: 'Arrival of Bomb' — Tilak's pen, not pistol, sent him to Mandalay.

FYQ 9

Consider the following statements about the operational distinction between the Jugantar and the Dacca Anushilan Samiti: 1. Answer: (A)

(A)

(A) ✓ — Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong — it was Jugantar that practised 'propaganda of the deed' (immediate spectacular assassinations); the Dacca Samiti was built for long-term military preparation & mass action.

Why others wrong: (b) statement 3 is wrong & statement 2 is correct; (c) statement 3 is wrong & statement 1 is correct; (d) statement 3 is wrong.

Memory hook: Jugantar = loud & loose; Dacca = quiet & coiled spring.

FYQ 11

With reference to the formal establishment of the Criminal Investigation Department (CID) by the colonial government, which one of the following statements is correct?

Answer: (A)

(A)

(A) ✓ — The colonial government formally established the CID in 1903, following the recommendations of the Fraser Police Commission of 1902 — a specialised intelligence apparatus beyond the foundational Indian Police Act of 1861, designed to track, infiltrate, and dismantle political dissent & secret societies.

Why others wrong: (b) the Indian Police Act of 1861 was foundational policing law — the CID was a separate specialised body set up later; (c) the CID precedes the Swadeshi Movement & was set up in 1903, not 1905; (d) the CID was already operating five years before the Alipore Case (1908).

Memory hook: Fraser Commission 1902 -> CID 1903 — the spy machine before the bombs.

FYQ 13

Why is any UPSC option linking Barindra Kumar Ghosh to the Ghadar Movement (1913–15) considered an elimination trap?

Answer: (B)

(B)

(B) ✓ — Barindra Kumar Ghosh was sentenced & transported to the Cellular Jail from 1908 onwards (after the Alipore Case). This means he could not have been a participant in the Ghadar Movement (1913–15) — any UPSC option linking him to Ghadar is an elimination trap, since once transported he was cut off from all revolutionary activity on the mainland.

Why others wrong: (a) Barindra did not die in the Muzzafarpur bombing — that was a separate event in which Chaki shot himself and Khudiram was hanged; (c) Barindra did not join the INC moderates — he was transported for life; (d) Aurobindo escaped to Pondicherry, not Barindra.

Memory hook: Barindra in Kala Pani from 1908 — cannot be in Ghadar's 1913 ship.

FYQ 15

How is Rash Behari Bose linked to the formation of the first Indian National Army (INA)?

Answer: (B)

(B)

(B) ✓ — After the Delhi Conspiracy Case (1912), Rash Behari Bose escaped to Japan in 1915 & later, in 1942, convened conferences in Tokyo & Bangkok to establish the Indian Independence League (IIL). It was under the political backing & guidance of his IIL that Captain Mohan Singh organised Indian POWs (captured by the Japanese) into the first INA.

Why others wrong: (a) Rash Behari Bose did not lead the INA into Imphal-Kohima — by then Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose led the reorganised Azad Hind Fauj; (c) the Ghadar Party in San Francisco was founded by Lala Hardayal, not Rash Behari Bose; (d) he handed over INA leadership to Subhas Chandra Bose in 1943, not directly in 1942, and after taking it over himself following Mohan Singh's arrest.

Memory hook: Rash Behari: Delhi 1912 -> Japan 1915 -> IIL 1942 -> hands INA baton to Netaji
