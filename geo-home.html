How this notebook works

Each of the six subjects has two chapters in the shelf: the subject chapter (1–6) and its mind maps (7–12).

Part What it holds
Exam lens Opens every subject chapter: priority and the PYQs the handout quotes.
Full notes The whole handout, line by line, in its original order. Bullets, bold, red and yellow highlights are kept. Every picture, map and table sits where the handout has it. Tap one to zoom.
Quick revise My layer on top: memory cards (mnemonics), PYQ tables with answers and handout alerts, then the recall sheet and traps at the end.
Mind maps Chapters 7–12: every mind-map page from your PDF for that subject, in order, zoomable, each with its own read mark.

🎯 Exam lens – priority and the PYQs the handout quotes.

🧠 Memory card – a picture, rhyme or acronym that carries the facts.

⚠️ Handout alert – where the handout contradicts itself. Use the stated rule.

Suggested way to study a chapter: read Full notes once (use "Jump to a topic"), then read the matching Quick revise cards, glance at the Mind maps, and from then on revise from the Quick recall sheet and mind maps only.

Memory methods used

Method What it does Where you will see it
Picture pegs Facts are hung on a familiar object (a 5-floor building for the atmosphere) Emoji headers on 🧠 cards
Acronyms and sentences First letters become a silly line (Hindi or English) Peaks, passes, tributaries, ports
Contrast pairs Two look-alikes side by side so the difference is the thing you store Tables marked "vs"
Number ladders Numbers placed in order so each cues the next 75–115–120 farming, road and rail numbers
Recall first Cover the answer, say it aloud, then check Recall sheets

Mnemonics marked teacher/handout come from the PDF. The others are my own pegs built only on handout facts. Replace any that do not click with your own; a self-made one is remembered best.
