1 · Article 350B and the meaning of linguistic minority#

The Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities is a constitutional authority under Article 350B. The provision was inserted by the Seventh Amendment Act of 1956 after the recommendations of the States Reorganisation Commission (SRC), 1956, which stressed the need to protect linguistic minorities when states were reorganised on linguistic lines. Art. 350B requires the President to appoint a Special Officer to investigate all matters relating to the safeguards for linguistic minorities and to report to the President periodically.

Who they are

  • groups in any part of India with a distinct language or script of their own

State level

  • groups whose mother tongue differs from the principal language of the state

District or tehsil level

  • groups whose language differs from that of the district or tehsil

Eighth Schedule

  • the minority language need not be one of the 22 listed languages
Key point

The Constitution does not define "linguistic minority"; the meaning is understood from context as above.

Origin chain
SSAB

SRC 1956, Seventh Amendment 1956, Article 350-B: "States Stirred, Amended, Backed minorities".

Origin shortcut

SRC 1956 recommends, 7th Amendment 1956 adds 350A and 350B. Both are 1956.

2 · Constitutional evolution: Articles 350A, 350B and 351#

Three articles in the handout
ArticleSubjectProvision
Art. 350AFacilities for instruction in mother tongue at primary stageEndeavour of every State and every local authority to provide adequate facilities for instruction in the mother tongue at the primary stage to children of linguistic minority groups; the President may issue directions to any State as he considers necessary
Art. 350BSpecial Officer for linguistic minoritiesSpecial Officer appointed by the President; duty to investigate all matters on safeguards for linguistic minorities and report to the President at such intervals as the President directs; the President causes all reports to be laid before each House of Parliament and sent to the governments of the States concerned
Art. 351Directive for development of the Hindi languageDuty of the Union to promote the spread of Hindi and develop it as a medium of expression for all elements of the composite culture of India, assimilating without interfering with its genius the forms, style and expressions used in Hindustani and in the other languages in the Eighth Schedule, and drawing for vocabulary on other languages where necessary or desirable
Eighth Schedule

At present the Eighth Schedule consists of 22 languages (originally 14).

Article trio
MSH

Article shortcut

A = mother tongue, B = Special Officer (boss), 351 = Hindi. 350A and 350B were added together in 1956.

3 · The Commissioner for Linguistic Minorities (CLM)#

The Commissioner for Linguistic Minorities (CLM) is the official title of the Special Officer. It was constituted in 1957 to monitor and report on implementation of the safeguards.

Where the office sat

1957
Constituted; initially based in New Delhi
Later
Shifted to Allahabad
2015
Relocated back to New Delhi to strengthen central coordination

The Commissioner's role goes beyond constitutional supervision to fostering cooperative engagement between linguistic groups and state governments.

Reporting to the PresidentPeriodic reports; the President lays them before both Houses of Parliament and the concerned state governments for review and action
Promotion of welfareOversees implementation of policies to protect the linguistic and cultural heritage of minority language groups

6 · Significance#

  • Protects the linguistic rights of about 36 million citizens who speak minority languages (Census 2011).
  • A vital link between the Union Government, States and linguistic communities, keeping diversity a cornerstone of national unity.
  • The Special Officer is the constitutional guardian of linguistic pluralism, ensuring minority rights and cultural identities are respected in India's democratic, multilingual society.
Key point

Think of the office as a link: Union, States and language communities in one chain.
