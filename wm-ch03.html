1 · Regions of Africa and the continent at a glance#

Class slides 43–47

Africa

  • North Africa: Algeria, Egypt, Libya, Morocco, Tunisia, and Sudan (sometimes considered part of Eastern Africa, but UN considers it to be in North Africa)
  • West Africa: Benin, Burkina Faso, Cabo Verde, Côte d’Ivoire, Gambia, Ghana, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Liberia, Mali, Mauritania, Niger, Nigeria, Senegal, Sierra Leone, Togo.
  • Central Africa: Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Congo (Rep.), DR Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, São Tomé & Príncipe.
  • East Africa: Burundi, Comoros, Djibouti, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Kenya, Madagascar, Malawi, Mauritius, Rwanda, Seychelles, Somalia, South Sudan, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe
  • Southern Africa: Botswana, Eswatini, Lesotho, Namibia, Mozambique, South Africa. The Horn of Africa is another prominent region in the Eastern part of Africa, including: Djibouti, Eritrea, Ethiopia, and Somalia (SEED).

The Great African Rift Valley

The Great Rift Valley extends for nearly 6,000 km from Lebanon in Southwest Asia to Mozambique in southeastern Africa. It is broadly divided into the Eastern Rift Valley, passing through countries such as Kenya and Tanzania, and the Western Rift Valley (Albertine Rift), which includes regions around Uganda and Rwanda.

From north to south, the rift system traverses Lebanon, Syria, Israel, Jordan, Ethiopia, Eritrea, Djibouti, Kenya, Tanzania, Somalia, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, the Democratic Republic of Congo, Zambia, Malawi, Sudan, and Mozambique, forming one of the world’s most prominent tectonic features.

Important Grasslands of Africa

Africa is endowed with both tropical and temperate grasslands. They include:

  • Savannahs: They are the world’s largest tropical grasslands, with the presence of grasses and scattered trees (acacia, baobab). It supports rich wildlife and pastoralism. Countries include: o West Africa: Senegal, Guinea-Bissau, Guinea, Sierra Leone, Ivory Coast, Ghana, Togo, Benin, Nigeria, Cameroon. o East/Central/Southern Africa: Sudan, South Sudan, Ethiopia, Somalia, Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania (Serengeti), Angola, Democratic Republic of Congo, Rwanda, Burundi, Malawi, Mozambique, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Botswana, Namibia, South Africa.
  • Velds: These are the temperate grasslands in the Southern part of Africa, across South Africa, Lesotho, Eswatini (Swaziland). This region is used for grazing and farming and includes subtypes like Highveld and Lowveld based on the altitude.

Traps#

Common mix-ups and the rule for the exam hall
TrapWhat goes wrong, and the rule
Atlas, Abyssinian, OkavangoAtlas = north-western Africa; Abyssinian plateau is in Ethiopia; Okavango basin is in southern Africa (PYQ 2013).
Cape of Good HopeSouthernmost point of Africa is Cape Agulhas, not the Cape of Good Hope (slide 46).
