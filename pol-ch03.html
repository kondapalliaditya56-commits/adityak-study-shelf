What to prepare

Prelims hooks: match each feature with its source: DPSP (Ireland), Fundamental Duties (USSR), Concurrent List (Australia), procedure established by law (Japan), amendment procedure (South Africa), emergency suspension of rights (Weimar), residuary powers (Canada).

Mains hooks: borrowed versus adapted (see Ch 2); which borrowed features fit India's federal and parliamentary design.

Closing idea: Ten countries and one Act gave the parts; the framers fitted them to India rather than copying them whole.

2 · Memory tricks for the table#

Countries in order
GBUICAWSFSJ

GoI Act 1935, Britain, USA, Ireland, Canada, Australia, Weimar, Soviet, France, South Africa, Japan: "Good Boys Usually Ignore Careless Aunts' Warnings; Some Friends Still Joke."

Feature-to-country shortcut

Rights and courts = USA (FR, judicial review, impeachment, VP). Government machinery = Britain (parliament, cabinet, Rule of Law, writs). Ideals of the State = Ireland (DPSP) and USSR (duties). Federal mix = Canada + Australia + GoI Act 1935.

Canada

  • Strong Centre
  • residuary powers with the Centre
  • Governors appointed by the Centre

Australia

  • Concurrent List
  • freedom of trade, commerce and intercourse
  • joint sitting of both Houses

GoI Act 1935

  • Federal scheme
  • Governor's office
  • Public Service Commissions
  • judiciary
  • emergency provisions
Ireland's three
DNE

DPSP, Nomination of Rajya Sabha members, Election method of the President: "Dublin Never Ends".

Single-feature countries
WSJ

Weimar = suspension of FRs in Emergency; South Africa = amendment procedure and RS election by STV; Japan = procedure established by law.

Handout alert

Single citizenship is credited to Britain here, while the Salient Features chapter describes it as a contrast with US dual citizenship. Do not mix them: for "source", answer Britain; for "model contrast", answer US.
