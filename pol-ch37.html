1 · Origin and constitutional status#

The National Commission for Backward Classes (NCBC) began under the National Commission for Backward Classes Act, 1993, a law of the Central Government to address the concerns of socially and educationally backward classes (SEBCs). It was a statutory body that examined the inclusion or exclusion of communities in the Central List of OBCs and advised the government. It was reconstituted several times to continue its role.

From statute to Constitution

1993
NCBC Act, 1993: statutory body for SEBCs; examines inclusion or exclusion in the Central List of OBCs
2018
NCBC (Repeal) Act, 2018 repeals the 1993 Act
2018
Constitution (One Hundred and Second Amendment) Act, 2018 inserts Article 338B: constitutional status
Today
NCBC is a constitutional body for SEBCs; ensures permanence and accountability
  • The NCBC functions under the administrative control of the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.
  • It is a key constitutional mechanism for equity, social justice and empowerment of backward classes.
From Act to Article
ARA

Act 1993, Repeal 2018, Article 338B (102nd Amendment): "A Real Advance".

Date shortcut

1993 Act, then both the Repeal Act and the 102nd Amendment in 2018. Article = 338B.

3 · Functions of the Commission#

Six functions
FunctionWhat it does
Investigation and monitoring of safeguardsInvestigates and monitors all matters on constitutional and other legal safeguards for SEBCs and evaluates their effectiveness
Inquiry into specific complaintsInquires into complaints of deprivation or violation of rights and safeguards (eg: non-implementation of reservation in government services or educational institutions)
Advisory role in developmentParticipates in and advises on socio-economic development; evaluates progress of welfare measures under Union and State governments
Reporting to the PresidentAnnual reports and additional reports as it deems necessary, assessing how safeguards and policies are implemented across India
RecommendationsTo Union and State governments on measures for better implementation and for overall protection and welfare of SEBCs
Additional functionsOther functions on protection, welfare, development and advancement as specified by the President
Six functions
IIARRA

Investigate, Inquire, Advise on development, Report, Recommend, Additional functions: "I I Am Ready, Really, Always".

Verb shortcut

Investigate, inquire, advise, report, recommend, plus whatever the President assigns.

5 · Reports of the Commission#

The NCBC prepares an annual report to the President and may table special reports whenever circumstances or inquiries warrant. These consolidate findings from monitoring, investigations and policy reviews, and are the primary written record by which the NCBC tells the executive and legislature about systemic gaps and remedies.

Where the report goes

Commission
Annual report and special reports to the President
President
Places each report before Parliament with a memorandum of action taken by the government
Not accepted
The memorandum must explain the reasons for non-acceptance

State matters

President
Forwards the report on matters concerning a State to the state government
State government
Places it before the State Legislature with its own action-taken memorandum and explanations for any non-acceptance
Accountability loop

The NCBC identifies problems and proposes measures; the executive records remedial action (or reasons for inaction) in a memorandum; Parliament and State Legislatures get material for legislative and public scrutiny.

Handout alert

For State matters the NCST chapter says the President forwards the report to the Governor, while this NCBC text says the state government. Use each handout's own wording for its chapter.

6 · Powers of the NCBC#

The Commission can frame and regulate its own procedure: how inquiries are conducted, how evidence is taken and how internal processes run. When investigating or inquiring into a complaint it has the procedural powers of a civil court:

Civil-court powers (a) to (f)
ItemPower
(a)Summon and enforce attendance of any person from any part of the country and examine on oath
(b)Require discovery and production of documents
(c)Receive evidence on affidavit
(d)Requisition public records or copies from any court or office
(e)Issue commissions for the examination of witnesses and documents
(f)Any other procedural power the President may specify by rule
Consultation duty

The Central Government and every State Government must consult the Commission on major policy matters affecting socially and educationally backward classes.

Six powers
SDARIO

Summon, Documents, Affidavit, Records, Issue commissions, Other by President's rule: "Six Different Actions Reveal Issues Openly".

Power shortcut

Same six civil-court powers as the NCST (a to f), plus own procedure and a consultation duty.
